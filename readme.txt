Soundgeon

Soundgeon is a fully blind-accessible, browser-based audio dungeon crawler. Built entirely without visual cues, players must rely on 3D spatial audio to navigate a grid-based labyrinth, track down healing resources, and battle monsters all the way down to the 99th floor.

The goal of this project is to provide a deep, strategic, classic RPG experience for visually impaired players without requiring screen-reading software.

🎧 Features
Spatial Audio Navigation: Follow the ambient hums of MP reserves, the rushing water of healing HP streams, and the distinct pings of treasure chests using dynamic stereo panning.

Classic Turn-Based Combat: Engage in tactical battles against goblins, orcs, and giant spiders. Enemy health and damage dynamically scale the deeper you go.

The Merchant: Spend your looted gold between floors to buy HP/MP potions or permanently upgrade your strength and max health.

The 99-Floor Challenge: Survive the descent to face the Dark Magician. Past the 10th floor, the boss will actively attempt to sabotage your run by stripping vital healing resources from the map.

Fully Voiced Interface: Every menu, stat check, environmental cue, and battle prompt is completely narrated.

🎮 How to Play
The game runs entirely in the browser and features intuitive inputs for both desktop and mobile devices. Stereo headphones are highly recommended for accurate spatial navigation.

Desktop Controls
Arrow Keys: Move forward, backward, turn left, or turn right. Navigate menus.

F Key: Interact with objects, open chests, use stairs, and confirm menu selections.

J Key: Open the Pause/Stats menu, cancel, or go back.

S Key: Skip the opening tutorial.

Mobile Touch Controls
Swipe (Up/Down/Left/Right): Move through the dungeon or scroll through menus.

Single-Finger Tap: Interact, open chests, use stairs, and confirm menu selections.

Two-Finger Tap: Open the Pause/Stats menu, cancel, or go back.

(Note for Mobile Users: You must physically tap the screen once to initialize the web audio engine. Ensure your device's physical ringer/silent switch is set to ON.)
